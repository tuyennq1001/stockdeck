What's New in StockDeck 1.1.29

  • Obsidian Slate Financial Terminal Theme: Introduced the sleek Obsidian Slate Dark Terminal aesthetic as default, featuring high-contrast typography, refined 4-tier surfaces, and micro-hairline borders.
  • Theme Selector in Settings: Choose effortlessly between Obsidian Slate (Terminal), Private Banking (Classic), or System in Settings > Appearance.
  • Dynamic Financial Accents: P&L gains and losses dynamically adapt to your selected theme — vibrant Emerald Mint and Crimson Coral on dark, and sober emerald with muted terracotta on classic paper light.
  • Unified Watchlist & Superchart: Integrated real-time Superchart directly into the Watchlist view alongside the detailed metrics table and Symbol Deep Dive support.

What's New in StockDeck 1.1.4

  • Portfolio Chart Inception Lock: Portfolio chart timeframes are now strictly anchored to your actual initial investment date, ensuring the "All" view accurately reflects true investment lifetime without arbitrary historical extensions.
  • Adaptive Timeframe Range Picker: Range options exceeding your portfolio's investment age (such as 3Y or 5Y) are automatically dimmed and disabled with helpful tooltips, preventing misleading backfill calculations.
  • Japanese Mutual Fund Pricing Standardization: Corrected average purchase price and valuation display for Japanese investment trusts to consistently reflect standard quotes per 10,000 units.

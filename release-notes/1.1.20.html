What's New in StockDeck 1.1.20

  • Periodic Local Auto-Export: Automatically export clean JSON snapshots of portfolios, active holdings, closed trades, and transaction history to iCloud Drive or local storage for third-party tools.
  • Configurable Schedule: Choose between bi-weekly (1st & 15th) and monthly (1st) auto-export schedules with automatic catch-up on application launch, plus manual export on demand.
  • Rich Metadata & Sanitized Payloads: Exported files include asset types, market exchange details, normalized account identifiers, realized P&L for closed positions, and missing cost basis flags for exchange-synced assets.

What's New in StockDeck 1.1.22

  • Superchart Drawing Tools: Add a TradingView-style left drawing toolbar with trendlines, arrows, horizontal/vertical lines, rays, rectangles, price/date ranges, price labels, rulers, and vector note markers.
  • Floating Drawing Toolbar & Inline Note Editor: Customize colors, font colors, font sizes, line widths (1px–4px), and line styles (solid, dashed, dotted), and edit notes directly on the chart.
  • Favorite Portfolios & Watchlists: Pin favorite portfolios and watchlists to the Superchart header bar with quick ⌘1–⌘9 keyboard shortcuts.

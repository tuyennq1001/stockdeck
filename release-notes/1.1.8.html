What's New in StockDeck 1.1.8

  • Accurate Performance Periods: Fixed an issue where newly listed stocks with limited trading history duplicated initial IPO returns across longer periods (such as 6M, YTD, 1Y, 2Y, 3Y). The app now accurately displays placeholder dashes (—) when historical data is insufficient.
  • Stock & Crypto Search Collision Prevention: Prevented symbol key collisions and unexpected quote overrides between stocks and crypto assets sharing identical identifiers.

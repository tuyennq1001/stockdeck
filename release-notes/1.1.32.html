What's New in StockDeck 1.1.32

  • Auto-Reconcile Stuck Sell Trades on Re-Import: Re-importing a standalone sell trade now automatically repairs any pre-existing unresolved closed trade placeholder (missing buy cost), deducts matching holdings in FIFO order, and populates the real buy price and realized P&L.
  • Legacy Account Fallback & Purchase Date Guard: Standalone sell matching now safely falls back to legacy or manually added lots without account info while never crossing named accounts or matching lots purchased after the sell date.

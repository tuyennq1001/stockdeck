What's New in StockDeck 1.1.23

  • Compact Price Range & Measurement Badges: Reduced default font size for Price Range, Date & Price Range, and Ruler tools to 10px semibold with compact padding, preventing large badges from obscuring candlesticks.
  • Smart Badge Placement: Measurement labels now position cleanly at the destination target edge (bottom on drops, top on rallies), keeping the interior price action completely visible.
  • Expanded Font Size Options: Added 9px and 10px options to the floating toolbar font size popup for flexible label styling.
  • Indicator Pane Persistence: Customized indicator pane sizes (RSI, MACD) now persist reliably across surfaces and cold restarts.

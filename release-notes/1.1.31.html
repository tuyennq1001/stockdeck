What's New in StockDeck 1.1.31

  • FIFO Deduction on Standalone Sell Imports: Importing a standalone sell transaction (without its original buy lot in the same file) now matches and deducts existing holdings in FIFO order and fills in the real buy price, buy date, and realized P&L.
  • Re-Import Deduplication: Re-importing the same sell transaction no longer double-deducts remaining holdings or creates duplicate closed trades.
